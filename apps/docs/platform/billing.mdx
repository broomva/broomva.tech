## Credits

Model and feature costs can vary. The current hosted behavior is a monthly
allocation with a hard stop when the available credits are exhausted. Credits
are not redeemable for cash. Internal billing-library policy types are not a
public offer and do not override Pricing, Checkout, or a signed agreement.

## Checkout and renewal

When re-enabled after validation, Pro checkout will be hosted by Stripe. Review the price, billing interval, automatic
renewal, cancellation timing, taxes, and applicable refund terms before paying.
Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Eligible subscriptions can be
managed through Stripe's Customer Portal.

Only organization owners and admins may start Checkout or open the billing
portal. Team and Enterprise are not available through public self-service.

## Payment and PCI boundary

Stripe-hosted Checkout handles card entry, and Broomva does not intend to store
full card numbers. This can reduce PCI scope; it does not by itself prove PCI
DSS compliance or remove the merchant's ongoing duties.

## Webhook status

Stripe webhook signatures are verified. Event-ID deduplication and
transactional, invoice-keyed credit grants remain open. Webhook processing must
not be described as idempotent until those controls are implemented and tested.

Mandatory withdrawal, refund, reversal, guarantee, and cancellation rights
remain available where applicable. See the public
[Terms](https://broomva.tech/terms), [Privacy](https://broomva.tech/privacy),
and [Pricing](https://broomva.tech/pricing) pages for the current offer.
