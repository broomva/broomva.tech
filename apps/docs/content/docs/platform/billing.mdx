# Billing & Plans

Broomva uses credits as internal usage units. Credits are not cash, stored
value, or a promise that one credit always equals a fixed currency amount.
Free registration remains available. New paid self-service checkout is
temporarily suspended pending operator, consumer-flow, and Stripe validation;
Pro, Team, and Enterprise require contact and a separately validated agreement.

## Credits

Credits measure usage against a plan allocation. Model and feature costs can
vary. The current public behavior is:

- Free receives 50 credits on initial registration or transition back from paid and does not reset monthly; Pro receives a monthly allocation.
- Usage pauses when the available allocation is exhausted.
- Unused credits do not roll over.
- Metered or invoice overage is not currently enabled.
- Credits are not redeemable for cash.

Internal billing libraries may contain experimental policy types or nominal
plan definitions. Those capabilities are not a public offer and do not override
the Pricing page, Checkout session, or signed customer agreement.

## Checkout and renewal

When re-enabled after validation, Pro checkout will be hosted by Stripe. Before confirming payment, review the price,
billing interval, automatic renewal, cancellation timing, taxes, and applicable
refund terms shown in Checkout. The worktree also requests Stripe's Terms of
Service consent control; it still requires an actual test-mode Checkout receipt
and the correct Stripe Dashboard public Terms URL before deployment.

Existing subscriptions renew under their agreed terms until canceled. The Customer Portal can manage or
cancel an eligible subscription. Access ordinarily continues through the paid
period, subject to mandatory law and the current Terms.

<Callout type="warn">
  “Non-refundable” is not absolute. Mandatory withdrawal, refund, reversal,
  guarantee, or cancellation rights remain available where applicable.
</Callout>

## Payment and PCI boundary

Stripe-hosted Checkout handles payment-card entry, and Broomva does not intend
to store full card numbers. Outsourcing card entry can reduce PCI scope; it does
not by itself prove PCI DSS compliance or remove the merchant's ongoing duties.

## Billing administration

Only organization owners and admins may start Checkout or open the billing
portal. Team and Enterprise are not available through public self-service.

## Webhook status

Stripe webhook signatures are verified. Event-ID deduplication and
transactional, invoice-keyed credit grants remain an open engineering control;
until that is implemented and tested, webhook processing must not be described
as idempotent.
